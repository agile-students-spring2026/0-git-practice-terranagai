### Article Discussion
Article link: https://aimagazine.com/news/globant-and-fifa-bringing-ai-to-football

What I find interesting about this article is that the integration of AI and data analytics in FIFA's biggest competitions is not only enhancing the overall viewing experience for fans and reducing error in live games from referees, but it is also leveling the playing field for all countries and clubs in big competitions. With every team given FIFA's advanced technology during elite tournaments, even smaller countries/clubs that have less funding or access to the highest quality technology are now able to receive thorough player, team, and game data analytics to help them plan their tactics and understand opponents. I think that will make the FIFA World Cup that is coming up to be more interesting, as we will see how every team takes advantage of the technology handed to them and will possibly see many upsets.
